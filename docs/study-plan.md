# OCP Java 21 study plan

About 2 hours a day, Monday to Friday, 4 Jan – 26 Feb 2027. Before January, 15 minutes of drills
twice a week keeps it warm.

Daily shape: 15 min quiz warm-up, 75 min drills or a kata, 15 min explaining one surprise to
Claude in English, 5 min `PROGRESS.md`.

| Week | Dates | Drills | Kata | Quiz domains |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4–8 Jan | `01-values`, `02-flow` | K01 shapes | values, flow |
| 2 | 11–15 Jan | `03-oop` | K02 money | oop |
| 3 | 18–22 Jan | `04-exceptions`, `05-collections` | K05 recent items | exc, coll |
| 4 | 25–29 Jan | `06-streams` | K03 order stats | streams |
| 5 | 1–5 Feb | `08-concurrency` | K04 price aggregator | conc |
| 6 | 8–12 Feb | `07-modules`, `09-io`, `10-localization` (write a two-module app by hand and run `jdeps`, `jlink`) | K06 receipts | modules, io, l10n |
| 7 | 15–19 Feb | `random 20` daily, redo every wrong drill | Redo the katas from scratch | Mock every other day |
| 8 | 22–26 Feb | Light review Mon–Wed, rest Thu | – | One mock Mon and Wed. **Exam Fri 26 Feb** |

Also buy a mock-exam pack with full-length tests (Enthuware is the usual choice for OCP) in week 5,
and book the exam once you score 80% on two full mocks.

## Exam facts (VALIDATE on Oracle's page when booking)
- 1Z0-830, 50 questions, 120 minutes, 68% to pass, online proctored.
- No paper: you get an on-screen notepad. A large monitor helps with long code.
